Text message program

Program name: ItGo.ai appointment messages.

What it is: Car detailing businesses use ItGo.ai to reply to customers by text, text back callers they missed, book and confirm appointments, send reminders, ask for reviews, and let past customers know when it may be time for their next detail.

How you join: By texting or calling a business that uses ItGo.ai, or by entering your phone number on a booking form and agreeing to receive appointment texts.

Message frequency: Varies based on your conversation and appointments.

Cost: Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out: Reply STOP to stop receiving messages. You'll get one final message confirming you've opted out. Reply START to join again.

Help: Reply HELP, or contact us using the details on our home page.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Appointments

Bookings made by text are requests until the business confirms them. Prices, times and services are set by each business, which may adjust the final price based on the condition of the vehicle.
