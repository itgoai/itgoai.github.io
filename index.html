Never miss another detailing customer.

ItGo.ai answers your texts in seconds, finds out what the customer needs, offers open times from your calendar, and sends you the booking to approve with one text.

How it works

A customer texts

They text the business number about a detail, any time of day.

The assistant replies

It asks about the car and the service, then offers open times.

You approve

You get the details by text. Reply YES and the customer is confirmed.

Text messages

How customers opt in. Customers start the conversation by texting a business that uses ItGo.ai, or by entering their phone number on a booking form and checking a box agreeing to receive appointment texts.

What we send. Replies to your questions, appointment confirmations, reminders, and a request for a review after your appointment. We do not send marketing to people who haven't contacted the business.

Frequency and cost. Message frequency varies based on your conversation and appointments. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
